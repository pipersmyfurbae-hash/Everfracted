The public projection stays intentionally small.

Moodoor can use

  • Title, summary, approved image, and price
  • Availability label (e.g., "In stock", "Limited")
  • Public mood, season, palette, and formula tags
  • Short, customer-safe match explanation

Moodoor never receives

  • Internal notes, supplier data, costs, and margins
  • Raw inventory quantities
  • Placement coordinates, collision data, and production detail
  • Internal score components and confidence values
Takeaway Customer-facing matching is useful without exposing maker operations.
