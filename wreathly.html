Feature 01 · Design DNA

Your style, made visible.

Design DNA captures the patterns that make a wreath feel like yours: the emotions you reach for, the formulas you trust, the palettes you return to, and the way you balance focal blooms against quiet space.

Instead of starting from a prompt every time, you start from a point of view. Wreathly turns that point of view into a reusable design language you can apply across an entire season.

Design DNA is living. Every saved design improves the pattern library behind your studio — so your next composition starts closer to your signature.

Feature 02 · Inventory intelligence

Know the cost before you cut.

Wreathly connects the visual idea to the physical materials on your shelf. Every stem has a role, a quantity, and a cost — so the moment a design takes shape, your margin does too.

Swap a focal bloom and watch the retail suggestion update. Add a ribbon and see the build estimate shift. The creative decision and the business decision stay in the same room.

Inventory-first by design. Wreathly never suggests a composition that ignores what you actually have available. Your next best design starts with your real materials.

Feature 04 · Batch generation

Build a season, not just a wreath.

When you have a new collection in mind, Wreathly makes the first pass generous. Generate a family of directions from one emotional brief, then keep only the ones that feel unmistakably yours.

Each variation keeps a shared design DNA while changing the palette, hero materials, and styling energy — perfect for testing a collection before you commit to a cut list.

Studio tier and above. Batch generation creates up to 12 coherent directions at once, with a shared DNA fingerprint and separate cost estimates.
